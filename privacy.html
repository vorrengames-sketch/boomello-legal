Privacy Policy — BOOMELLO — Say Hello to the Boom!
A VORREN Game · Last updated: November 9, 2026

This policy explains what information BOOMELLO (the "Game") by VORREN Studios ("we", "us") handles and why. In short: we do not run any server for the Game and we do not receive your personal information. Some information is processed by the services built into the Game, mainly Google AdMob, Google Play and, for players 13 and older who choose it, Google Play Games, as described below. VORREN Studios is responsible for how the Game handles this information.

1. What stays on your device

The Game does not require an account. Your progress, coins, gems, unlocked items, settings, language and the player name you choose are saved only on your device. We do not receive them (see section 4 for the optional cloud save). We also keep on your device the age group you selected (under 13, 13–17 or 18+), never your date of birth.

2. Ads

The Game shows optional rewarded ads, only when you choose to watch one to get a bonus. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device and app information and ad interaction data. This is governed by Google's own policies (policies.google.com/privacy). You can reset or disable your advertising ID in your device settings. Ads are handled differently by age, see section 5. Where required (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), adult players are asked for their consent before personalized ads are shown.

3. Purchases

Optional in-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. Google Play keeps the purchase records under its own policies, and the Game uses them to deliver and restore what you bought.

4. Cloud save (Google Play Games, ages 13+)

Players aged 13 and older can keep their progress in the cloud with Google Play Games. If you use it, your game progress (coins, gems, unlocked items, stars, settings and player name) is saved in your Google Play Games account by Google. We do not receive it and cannot read it. Google processes it under its own privacy policy, and we do not use Play Games data for advertising. You are signed in with the Google account already on your device; to switch account, change it in your device or Google Play Games settings. Players under 13, and players whose age is unknown, are not offered cloud save and are not signed in to Google Play Games by the Game. You can delete cloud-saved data from the Google Play Games settings of your Google account.

4b. Analytics and online features

This version of the Game has no online multiplayer, no leaderboards with other players, no chat and no analytics service of our own. We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for advertising beyond the ad service described in sections 2 and 5.

5. Age screen and children

The Game is made for players aged 9 and up, including children. When you first open the Game we ask for your date of birth on a neutral screen. It is used only on your device to work out an age group (under 13, 13–17 or 18+) and is not stored or sent anywhere: only the age group is kept on your device. Until an age is entered, the Game does not start any ad service.

For players under 13, and for anyone whose age is unknown, ads are set for child-directed treatment: no personalized ads, ads limited to the "General audiences" content level, and no consent form for personalized advertising. Players aged 13–17 get non-personalized ads with a reduced content level, and a reduced ad content rating. Adults may see personalized ads only after giving consent where the law requires it. We do not collect personal information from children ourselves, and the Game has no account, chat or social features. Google's ad service may still process technical information such as the IP address and device identifiers to deliver and measure non-personalized ads; for child-directed treatment this is done under Google's rules for children. If you are a parent and have a concern, contact us and we will help.

6. Security, retention and deletion

Game data is protected by the Android app sandbox on your device and is not transmitted by us. We keep no game data on servers, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Data on the device stays until you remove it. "Reset progress" in Settings deletes your progress, coins, gems, unlocked items, settings and player name; the age group stays until you uninstall the Game. Uninstalling removes the Game's data from your device, but if Android or Google backup is turned on for your device, a copy may be kept in your Google account and restored if you reinstall; you can manage or delete backups in your Google account and device settings. Data processed by Google (ads, Google Play Games, Google Play purchase records) is kept by Google under its own policies, and may be transferred to and processed in countries outside yours under Google's safeguards. No method of storage is completely secure.

7. Your choices and rights

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Because the Game keeps no accounts and no server data, there is none to disclose or sell; if you are a parent and believe your child gave us personal information, contact us and we will delete it. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/EEA/UK or the laws of US states), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, to object, and to withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawing consent for ads can be done in the Game's privacy options when shown, and in your Google account ad settings. You can also complain to your local data protection authority. Because we do not keep your game data on our servers, the simplest way to remove it is to reset progress or uninstall the Game. For anything else, contact us.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
